# Kenya 2060: Regional Industrial Co-Reliance Framework

**One Kenya. Forty-Seven Contributors. Shared Prosperity.**

## Overview
This repository hosts the official open-access working files, economic data models, and policy whitepapers for the **Kenya 2060 All-Inclusive Development Proposal** (also known as the *Kenya 2060 Regional Industrial Co-Reliance Framework*)[cite: 1, 2]. The framework outlines a long-term structural transformation roadmap designed to transition Kenya from raw-material extraction to county-based industrial specialization, regional co-reliance, and rigorous public accountability[cite: 1, 2].

## Key Pillars
1. **Constitutional Alignment**: Operationalizing Articles 10, 43, 174, and 201 of the Constitution of Kenya (2010) to guarantee public participation, bulletproof governance against court injunctions, and prudent public finance[cite: 2].
2. **Nine-Region, 47-County Architecture**: Decentralized industrial mandates assigning a distinct economic role to every county across nine functional economic zones[cite: 1, 2].
3. **Infrastructure-to-Industry Mandate**: Preventing wasteful expenditure by coupling major infrastructure (ports, SEZs, corridors) with pre-committed industrial growth (modeled after integrated platforms like Morocco's Tanger Med and China's export-led spatial sequencing)[cite: 2].
4. **2030 Social Infrastructure Milestone**: Population-tiered service delivery standards for education, healthcare, and public infrastructure[cite: 2].
