# Constitutional Alignment & Bulletproof Governance

To prevent the legal vulnerabilities and court injunctions that have historically stalled major national infrastructure projects—such as disputes surrounding infrastructure financing funds—the Kenya 2060 framework embeds pre-emptive legal compliance:

1. **Strict Adherence to Public Finance Regulations**: Unlike attempts to structure capital funds through executive fiat or limited liability company models that trigger public mistrust and court suspensions, this framework mandates explicit parliamentary legislation and transparent asset disclosures from inception.
2. **Mandatory Public Participation at County Level**: By decentralizing the planning process down to all 47 counties through the nine-region framework[cite: 2], local communities actively co-author their industrial hubs, eliminating grounds for citizen petitions or lawsuits based on marginalization or lack of consultation.
3. **Fulfillment of Article 10 and Article 201**: Direct alignment with national values, public participation mandates, and the prudent allocation of public funds[cite: 2].
